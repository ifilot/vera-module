# Contributing

Thank you for helping Open VERA Module.

By submitting a pull request or other contribution, you confirm that you have
the right to contribute it and grant the project a non-exclusive right to
distribute it under the licence applicable to the paths you change:

- hardware design (`pcb/`, `firmware/source/`, constraints, and HDL design
  documentation): `CERN-OHL-S-2.0`;
- software (`driver/`, `toolchain/`, scripts, and CI): `GPL-3.0-or-later`.

You retain copyright in your contribution.  Please add a `Signed-off-by:`
line to every commit (for example, `git commit -s`) to certify this statement.
Do not submit third-party material unless its licence is compatible and its
notice and source are included.  Tell maintainers about imported CAD models,
fonts, generated files, and documentation so their notices can be retained.

Pre-existing upstream material remains subject to its recorded licence.  See
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) before changing a file with an existing notice.
